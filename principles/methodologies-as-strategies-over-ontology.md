# Methodologies Are Strategies Over Ontology

Methodologies do not define the world.

They are strategies for moving work through a model of the world.

PMBOK, Scrum, Kanban, SAFe, and similar methods answer execution questions: how to plan, sequence, govern, limit, review, and deliver work.

ODPM answers the question underneath:

> What shared model of reality are those methods executing against?

## The Principle

ODPM is not another methodology on the same shelf.

It is the base ontology those methodologies operate over.

Different methodologies optimize different things:

- PMBOK emphasizes constraints, governance, and control.
- Scrum emphasizes cadence, feedback, and state change.
- Kanban emphasizes flow and work-in-progress.
- SAFe emphasizes coordination across decomposition at scale.

Those strategies can all be useful.

But each one works better when the ontology beneath it is explicit.

## Why This Matters

Without a shared ontology, methodologies coordinate private interpretations.

With a shared ontology, methodologies execute against the same world.

ODPM clarifies the world before, during, and after execution.
