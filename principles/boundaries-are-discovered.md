# Boundaries Are Discovered, Not Designed

Ontology boundaries are findings, not preferences.

A team does not split an ontology because the diagram looks cleaner, the org chart suggests it, or a tool wants separate spaces.

A boundary earns its place when Chase Understanding reveals evidence that one shared model is no longer honest.

## The Test

Two conditions should both be present:

1. **Semantic divergence** — shared terms mean different things on each side.
2. **Independent evolution** — the domains change at different rates.

One condition is not enough.

Semantic divergence alone calls for disambiguation.

Independent evolution alone calls for versioning discipline.

Both together justify a boundary with an explicit mapping.

## Why This Matters

Boundaries drawn too early fragment the ontology.

Boundaries drawn too late force different worlds to share one vocabulary.

ODPM treats a boundary as evidence discovered through understanding, not a design choice imposed ahead of it.
