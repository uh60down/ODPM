# Done Means Verified

Done means verified, never merely claimed.

A work item is not done because someone says it is complete.

It is done when reality has been checked against the understanding the work was meant to realize.

## What Verification Means

Verification asks:

- Does the Thing exist as intended?
- Does the Relationship actually hold?
- Does the Rule resist violation?
- Are the States reachable and constrained as agreed?
- Do the Actions work with the right actor and guard?

The form of evidence can vary: a test, demo, review, production observation, or explicit human check.

The invariant is the same: the claim must be checked against reality.

## Why This Matters

Status can become theater.

Tasks can be marked complete while the shared model remains unrealized.

ODPM treats progress as verified ontology coverage: which parts of the agreed model are real now?
